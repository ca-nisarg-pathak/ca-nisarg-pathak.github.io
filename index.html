About the Firm

Ledgers are facts.
Advice is judgement.

We built our practice on the belief that accounting should give clients clarity, not just compliance.

A firm built around your numbers, not the other way around

Nisarg Pathak & Co. was founded to give individuals, startups, and established businesses a chartered accountancy partner who treats their finances with the same care as their own. We combine rigorous compliance work with genuinely useful financial advice — the kind that helps you make better decisions, not just pass an audit.

Our approach is direct: understand the business first, then build the numbers around it. Every engagement — from a first-time tax return to a full statutory audit — gets the same level of attention.
